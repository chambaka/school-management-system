ShuleHub teacher

What you can open after a teacher signs in: today’s classes, the register, assignments, exam marks, and the reports you are allowed to show and download.

Teacher login · October 2026

Sign in

  1. Open the school portal.
  2. Enter the email, phone, or username the school gave you.
  3. Enter your password.
  4. Click Login.

A correct teacher login opens Home. The greeting is “Habari” and your name, with your photo when one is on the staff record. Under the name is your department and employee ID.

Five wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes. Forgot password sends an SMS code to the phone on the account.

This guide is for a teacher login. You teach the subjects you are allocated to. You do not create exams, verify or publish results, generate the timetable, admit students, or open Finance, Users, or Audit. The academic master or headmaster does that work.

Your name is at the top. Sign out is at the bottom and asks you to confirm. On a phone, the first items stay on the bottom bar and the rest sit under More.

Home

Three counts sit under the greeting: Classes today, Unread alerts, and Notices.

Today’s classes lists the lessons on your timetable for today. If the day is empty, it says “No classes scheduled today.” Under the list are three buttons:

  • Mark attendance opens the student register.
  • Enter grades opens the exam marks grid.
  • Assignments opens homework.

Alerts shows assignment posted, up to five assignment submissions, then other unread alerts such as results published. Each line has a date. All alerts opens the Alerts page, which lists every alert and has Mark read on unread rows. It does not open Reports.

Notices shows the first four titles on the board. Message a parent opens Messages.

What you can do only if you are allocated

An allocation is the academic master’s link of you to a subject, class, year, and optional section. That link decides the work you can start.

  • You can create an assignment only for a class and subject you are allocated to. If you have no allocation, the page says only the allocated teacher can create assignments.
  • You can add an exam paper only for a subject you teach in that class.
  • You can save exam marks, and continuous-assessment marks, only for a subject you teach in that class. Otherwise the page says only the allocated teacher can submit marks.

Students

The gold label above the heading is Directory and admission. Search the directory, and sort with the Name, Email, and Status controls.

This login is for looking up a class. Admit, edit, link a parent, promote, unlock, and reset password stay with the headmaster, school admin, and academic master. Enrolment history on Reports is how you see who was promoted, repeated, graduated, or transferred.

Timetable

Choose the year and the section. The week grid shows each lesson in its bell period: subject, and the time. Cells are for reading. You cannot click a cell to add or edit a lesson, and you do not see Generate week, Bell times, or Teacher hours. Those stay with the headmaster and the academic master.

If the timetable is locked, everyone sees it as locked. Your Home page still uses the same lessons for “Classes today.”

Attendance

Two tabs: Students and Lesson logs. The staff register is not on a teacher login.

Students

  1. Choose the date and the section.
  2. Leave Lesson as Whole day, or pick one lesson from the timetable.
  3. Search if the class is long. Mark each student Present, Late, Absent, or Excused.
  4. Click Save register and confirm.

The confirm step says absent students will trigger a parent SMS. Late is notified the same way, when that parent still receives SMS and has a phone. A parent who chose in-app only gets the notice in the portal instead of a text.

Lesson logs

Choose the same date and section, then log what was taught: subject, topic, and optional objectives, materials, and homework. Save log asks you to confirm. The list under the form is that day’s logs. Search filters the list you are looking at.

Assignments

The page subtitle is “Homework and continuous assessment.” Search filters the cards. An assignment moves from Draft to Locked to Published.

Create and publish

  1. Click Add assignment. The class and subject lists are the ones you are allocated to.
  2. Enter the title, instructions, and due date. You can attach a document for the class. Save. A new assignment is a draft.
  3. While it is a draft you can edit it or delete it.
  4. Click Lock when it is ready. After lock, you cannot edit or delete it. The card says only the academic master can edit or delete it from then on.
  5. Click Publish on a locked assignment you teach. Students and parents in that class see it, and an “Assignment posted” alert is raised. The posted alert stays as one alert. Each student submission is its own alert, with its own date.

The refresh icon on Documents reloads the files. The refresh icon on Submissions reloads the work students have handed in.

Mark the work

On a published assignment, open submissions. If you teach that subject the button is Enter CA marks. Otherwise it is View work.

  • Only students who have submitted appear. A student who has not handed work in is not in the list.
  • Each student can have more than one attempt. The latest file can be viewed or downloaded. The attempt line uses the gold attempt label.
  • Save marks is on that student’s submission. Confirm “Save marks for” that student. It does not save the whole class at once.

Exams

Choose the academic year. Each exam shows the name, type, term, dates, class, and status: draft, entered, verified, approved, or published. Schedule locked appears when the academic master has locked the papers.

You do not create the exam, and you do not verify, approve, publish, unpublish, reject, lock the schedule, or delete it.

  1. Open Papers. You can add a paper only for a subject you are allocated to teach in that class, and only while the schedule is not locked. If none of your subjects match, the page says only the allocated teacher can add papers.
  2. Enter the marks on Grades.
  3. When the exam is still a draft, or it has been sent back, click Send for checking. The button stays off until the exam has papers and the marks are entered. Confirm. The exam then waits for the academic master or headmaster to verify.
Marks freeze when the exam is verified. You cannot change them on Grades until someone with exam control rejects the exam and sends it back. Publish is what students and parents see. You do not publish. After publish, students and parents get “Results published,” and parents who still receive SMS are texted if they have a phone.

Grades

Choose the exam and the subject. The line under the filters shows the subject average, the maximum, and the pass mark. The grid columns are admission number, student, marks, grade, and position.

  • Type marks in the row, then Save grid. Confirm “Save this marks grid?”
  • The trash icon on a row deletes that student’s marks, while the grid is still open.
  • Download Excel template is the sheet for those marks. Import Excel brings a filled sheet back in. Import is off when marks are frozen.
  • Search filters the students on the grid.

If you are not the allocated teacher, the marks boxes are not yours to save and the page says only the allocated teacher of this subject for this class can submit marks. After verification the page says marks are frozen. Reject the exam on Exams, which the academic master or headmaster does, to unlock them.

Term results

Term Results opens the combined scores for a year, class, and subject. The combined score uses midterm, semi exam, semi result, and terminal exam. A paper that is missing or not published counts as 0.

Reports

Download stays locked until the list on the screen matches the filters you are looking at. Change a filter and you must click Show again before PDF, CSV, or Excel will run.

ReportWhat it shows
Enrolment historyPromote, repeat, graduate, and transfer. The class and year are the placement at the time of that action, which is the class they left. Admitting a student does not create a row. Filter by student, year, or action, then Show. PDF and CSV / Excel use that list.
Term resultThe combined midterm, semi-terminal, and terminal scores for one student or for every active student.

AlertsThe notification list at the bottom of Reports, with Mark read on an unread row. Home → All alerts is the separate Alerts page, which is the full list only.

Messages

Choose a student. The thread is comments about that student. Parents of the student can read the note and reply. Write comment posts your note. The checkbox that texts parents is not shown for a teacher. A parent who should get SMS from the school still gets attendance texts and result texts through those flows, not from this box.

Notices

Notices are the school board. They are not the same as alerts, and publishing one does not send SMS or email.

  1. Enter a title and a body.
  2. Choose the audience: ALL, TEACHERS, STUDENTS, PARENTS, or STAFF.
  3. Click Publish notice and confirm.

A person must be signed in to read notices. There is no edit or delete on the board after you publish. As a teacher you see published notices for All, Teachers, and Staff. Search filters the list you are looking at. Home shows the first four titles.

Security

Enter the current password and a new one that meets the strength rules, then Update password. Other sessions are signed out.

Under Notification channels, click In-app or SMS. The one you click stays selected.

  • In-app keeps your alerts in the portal and stops SMS for your account.
  • SMS sends your alerts by text and stops the in-app copy.

Email and push are not offered. Until you choose, alerts stay as they are today: in the app, and by SMS where the school already texts. Reset 2FA is not on this page for a teacher. A headmaster or school admin resets an authenticator.

A normal teaching day

  1. Sign in and read Home: today’s classes, unread alerts, and the latest notices.
  2. Open Mark attendance for the section you are about to teach, or for the whole day, and save the register.
  3. Log the lesson: subject, topic, and homework if you set any.
  4. On Assignments, lock a draft that is ready, publish it, and later save marks only for students who submitted.
  5. On Grades, enter marks for your subject, save the grid, then on Exams send the exam for checking.
  6. Use Reports when you need a term result, a report card, or a merit list. Show the list before you download it.
  7. Sign out on a shared computer.
