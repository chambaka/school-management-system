Headmaster guide · How to set papers, the sitting plan, and results

Sign in as Headmaster → left menu → Exams

Create · Papers · Schedule · Lock · Check · Publish

Who this is for

You and the Academic master create exams, schedule them, lock the plan, approve, and publish. Teachers can add papers and enter marks. The Academic master is the one who verifies marks. School admin and invigilators can open the list, papers, and seats. They do not create exams or publish results.

Set up the academic year, classes, subjects, rooms, and teachers before exam week. Marks are entered on Grades, not on this page. This page builds the exam and releases the results.

Open the page

1 Sign in with your Headmaster email or phone.
2 If you have more than one school, pick the school first (Schools).
3 Click Exams. The subtitle is “Exams and papers for the selected year.” The year chip at the top opens on the current year.

Create an exam

1 Click Create exam.
2 Choose the year. Term can stay None.
3 Choose one class, or All classes. All classes creates one exam per class. Each class keeps its own papers and marks.
4 Name it (Midterm is the suggested name). Set start and end dates. The end date must be on or after the start date.
5 Choose Type and Component (see below).
6 Click Create exam and confirm.

The pen icon edits that exam. You can change year, term, class, name, type, component, and dates. Edit is for one class. It does not offer All classes.

Type and component

Type is the label on the row: Quiz, Midterm, Final, Assignment, or Other. Component is what the report uses. Pick the component that matches the result you want, even if the type name is similar.

ComponentHow it counts
MIDTERMThe midterm score. It is 10% of the semi-terminal result.
SEMI_TERMINALThe semi exam. It is 90% of the semi-terminal result.
TERMINALThe end-of-term exam. The term result is half the semi-terminal result and half this exam.
OTHERStored on the exam. It is not one of those three report parts.

Add a paper

A paper is one subject sitting: Maths, English, and so on. Seats are the places for the students in that sitting. You add papers first. Seat numbers appear after Schedule.

1 On the exam row, click Papers (0) or Papers (n).
2 Click Add paper.
3 Choose the subject. Max and pass are required. They start at 100 and 40.
4 Choose an Invigilator. This is required. The invigilator is the person supervising that sitting. The list is your teachers. A teacher who teaches the subject and the invigilator on the day can be different people.
5 Room, date, start, and end are optional. Room is a dropdown of rooms from Academics → Rooms. Choose No room if you will let Schedule pick one. If the room list is empty, add rooms under Academics first.
6 Click Add paper and confirm. One subject can appear only once on the same exam.

The pen on a paper edits it. The trash deletes it. Delete is refused if that paper already has marks, or if the exam is published. Unpublish first, and clear those marks, then delete.

While the schedule is locked, Add paper, edit, and delete are hidden. Unlock the exam before you change papers.

Schedule

Schedule builds the sitting plan and the seat numbers. It does not enter marks and it does not change Draft, Verified, or Published.

1 Add at least one paper. Schedule stays grey until then. The tooltip says “Add a paper before using this”.
2 Click Schedule and confirm “Auto-schedule papers”.
3 Open Papers. The Schedule table shows subject, date, time, room, and invigilator.

For a paper that does not yet have a date and a start time, Schedule walks the exam from the start date:

  • Morning 08:00–10:00, then afternoon 11:00–12:30, then the next weekday. Saturday and Sunday are skipped.
  • The date never goes past the exam end date.
  • The room is a classroom that can hold the active students in that class. If the school has no rooms, the room is Hall A.
  • The invigilator is taken from the teacher list, in turn, so two papers at the same time do not share a person or a room.

A paper that already has a date and a start time keeps that date, time, room, and invigilator. Schedule still rebuilds the seat numbers for it. Run Schedule again to replace the plan. You cannot run it while the exam is locked.

Seat numbers are only saved by Schedule. They are S-01, S-02, and so on, for active students in the exam’s class. The exam is for the whole class, not one section.

See the plan and the seats

  1. Click Papers. The Schedule table is at the top of that window.
  2. Click Seats on a paper.
  3. After Schedule, you see seat, admission number, and student.
  4. Before Schedule, you see the active students in the class, and a note that seat numbers are saved when you run Schedule.

The class timetable (lessons) is a different screen. The exam plan lives here, inside Papers.

Lock and unlock

Lock

Grey until every paper has a date and a start time. Hover says “Schedule the papers before locking” until then. After lock, the row shows Schedule locked. Papers cannot be added or edited. Schedule stays grey until you unlock.

Unlock

Replaces Lock while the plan is locked. Confirm unlock. Papers can be changed again, and you can run Schedule again.

You can lock a draft. Lock does not publish results. It only freezes the sitting plan.

Marks, checking, and publish

Do this after the papers exist. Schedule and lock can come before or after marks. The result steps themselves follow this order. A later button is hidden until the exam reaches that status. It is not a grey button you are meant to force.

1 On Grades, enter marks for the exam. Send for checking stays grey until at least one mark exists. Hover says “Enter marks on Grades before sending for checking”.
2 Click Send for checking. This does not save marks. It asks the Academic master to look. Marks can still be changed until the exam is verified. The status becomes Entered.
3 The Academic master clicks Verify. You do not see Verify. After that, marks are frozen and the status is Verified.
4 Click Approve. It appears only when the status is Verified.
5 Click Publish. It appears only when the status is Approved. Students and parents can then see the results.

Reject appears once the exam has been sent for checking. It returns the exam to the teacher. If results were published, Reject also hides them. The note “Returned for correction” shows under the row. Send for checking is available again after new marks, labelled as sending again.

Unpublish hides results from students and parents without returning the exam to the teacher. The status stays Approved until you publish again.

Delete an exam

  1. If it is published, click Unpublish first.
  2. Remove marks for that exam on Grades.
  3. Click the trash icon and confirm.

Delete also removes the papers and the seat numbers. It is refused while the exam is published or while any mark remains.
