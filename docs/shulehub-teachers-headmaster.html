ShuleHub Teachers menu

Headmaster guide · How to run the teaching staff list

Sign in as Headmaster → left menu → Teachers

List · Add teacher · Photo · Keep login safe

Who this is for

This page is the teaching staff directory. As Headmaster you can add, edit, deactivate, and help teachers sign in. School admin and Academic master can do the same.

Use Teachers only for class teachers. Non-teaching staff (office, support, invigilators) are added from Schools → Officers, not here.

Open the page

1 Sign in with your Headmaster email or phone.
2 If you have more than one school, pick the school first (Schools → Open school).
3 Click Teachers in the left menu. Title: “Teachers”. Subtitle: “Staff directory”.

Add a teacher

1 Click the gold Add teacher button. The form opens over the list.
2 Enter Name, Employee ID, and Email. Employee ID must be unique at this school.
3 Enter a phone number (Tanzania format). If you leave password blank, ShuleHub creates a temporary password and tries to SMS it to that phone.
4 Optional: choose Department and Qualification from the lists, and add specialization. Qualification names come from platform Configurations.
5 Optional: upload a photo (JPEG, PNG, or WebP, max 2 MB).
6 Click Save teacher and confirm. The form closes. They appear in the list and on Users with role Teacher.
Save stays disabled until there is a phone, or a password that meets the strength bar. Close, Cancel, Escape, or a click outside the form closes it without saving. Email cannot be changed after save — edit the rest of the record instead. Employee ID stays on the edit header and is not changed later.

Edit a teacher

  1. Click the pen icon on the row. The same form opens, titled Edit and their name.
  2. Change name, phone, department, qualification, specialization, or photo.
  3. Click Save changes and confirm.

Email stays locked. Employee ID is shown under the title so you can see who you are editing.

Photo

  • On Add teacher, choose a file before you save. It uploads after the teacher is created.
  • On Edit, Change photo uploads at once. Remove photo needs confirm.
  • Use JPEG, PNG, or WebP, 2 MB or smaller.

Keep a teacher login safe

Unlock

Shows when the row has a Locked chip (five failed logins). Confirm Unlock so they can sign in again at once.

Reset password

Creates a temporary password. If they have a phone, ShuleHub sends it by SMS. Confirm before you click.

Reset 2FA

Shows only if they enrolled Google Authenticator. After reset they set up 2FA again at next sign-in.

Deactivate

Archives the teacher. They leave the normal list. Data stays. Tick Show archived to find them, then Restore.

You can also Unlock, Reset password, and Reset 2FA on the Users page. Teachers is the place to add and edit the record.

Departments

The Department list comes from Academics. If it is empty, the school still has default departments in most cases. You can leave Department as None and set it later.

If something fails

What you seeWhat to do
Cannot save a new teacherNeed name, employee ID, email, and a phone or a password that meets the strength bar.
Employee ID already existsUse another ID. IDs cannot repeat at the same school.
Email is already registeredThat email is in use. Use another email.
Photo rejected / saved but photo failedTeacher is still created. Open Edit and upload JPEG/PNG/WebP under 2 MB.
“No SMS was sent”Teacher still saved or password still reset. Check the phone, or tell them the password in person.
Department list emptyLeave None, or add departments under Academics, then edit the teacher.
Add teacher missingOnly Headmaster, School admin, and Academic master see the button.
Need a non-teaching loginDo not use Teachers. Schools → Officers → Role Staff (or Invigilator).
