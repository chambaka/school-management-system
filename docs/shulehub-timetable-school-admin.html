Who this is for

This guide is for the school office. It explains the whole Timetable screen: what each control does, what must exist first, and what happens when a week is generated, edited, or locked. School admin can open the week and read it. Building and changing the week is done by Headmaster or Academic master.

The Timetable menu is the class week. It is not Academics (years, classes, subjects, rooms, who teaches what), not Attendance, and not the teacher’s personal home. Those menus feed this one.

How the pieces fit together

Year → Class → Section → this week’s grid

Subject + Teacher + Allocation → Generate week, or Add lesson by hand

Bell times (school-wide) → rows on every class week

Teacher hours (optional, school-wide) → where Generate is allowed to place that teacher

Room (optional) → named on the cell, and blocked if already booked

Open the page and read the screen

1 Sign in. If the account can open more than one school, open the correct school first. The timetable never mixes schools.
2 Click Timetable in the left menu.
3 The heading says Timetable. Under it: “One week for one class section. Rows are periods. A filled cell is a lesson.”

Teacher, Staff, and Invigilator can also open this menu and read a section’s week. They do not get Generate, Lock, Bell times, or Teacher hours. Students and parents do not see this menu. A student sees today’s lessons on their own home. A teacher sees their own lessons on the teacher home.

If Add lesson, Bell times, or Teacher hours are missing, the signed-in role is view-only on this page. Ask the Headmaster or Academic master to make the change.

What must exist first

The week cannot be filled until Academics and the staff list are in place. Do these on the other menus, in this order.

1 Academics → Years & terms — a year exists, and the one you want is available in the Year dropdown.
2 Academics → Classes — a class in that year, and at least one section (A, B).
3 Academics → Subjects — each subject you will place (Mathematics, English).
4 Teachers menu — the people who will stand in the cells.
5 Academics → Teachers — allocate teacher + subject + class + year. Section can be “whole class” or one stream. Generate uses these links. Add lesson can still pick any subject and teacher even without an allocation.
6 Academics → Rooms — optional. Buildings, then classrooms. The lesson form’s Room list is these names. You can leave a lesson with no room.
7 Timetable → Bell times — optional before the first look. Until someone saves bells, every class week uses the default day below.

Until a section is chosen, the Week tab shows an empty message instead of a grid. Typical messages:

  • “Add a school year in Academics first.” — no years.
  • “Add a class and a section (A, B) in Academics first.” — no sections.
  • “Pick a class section to see its week.” — year exists, nothing selected.
  • “No lessons yet. Allocate teachers to this class in Academics → Teachers, then generate — or click an empty cell.” — section chosen, no lessons, and no allocations for that class.
  • “No lessons yet. Generate from allocations, or click an empty cell to add one.” — allocations exist, the week is still empty.
  • “No lessons on this week yet.” — you are view-only and the week is empty.

Week grid

After you pick a section, the card title is the class and section (Form 1 A) and the year. The line under it says whether a click will add or edit a lesson, or only view it. View is used when the week is locked, or when your role cannot edit.

Part of the gridWhat you see
CornerThe word Period.
Day headersMon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri.
Left of each rowPeriod name and clock time, for example P1 and 07:30–08:10. Only lesson periods are rows. Breaks are not rows.
Empty cellBlank, with a plus if you can edit. Click it to add a lesson already set to that day and that period’s start and end.

A cell matches a lesson when the lesson’s day and start time are the same as that period. If a lesson was typed with a start time that matches no bell, it still counts in the lesson total, but it will not sit in a row.

If the school has no lesson bells and no saved lessons, the grid can build rows from the start times of lessons already saved, named P1, P2, and so on in time order.

Add, edit, and delete a lesson

Headmaster and Academic master. The week must be unlocked, and a section must be selected for Add lesson. A click on a cell is the same form.

Add

1 Click Add lesson, or click an empty cell. The form title is Add lesson. The note says a lesson is one slot: subject, teacher, day, and period time.
2 Subject — required. List of school subjects.
3 Teacher — required. List of teachers.
4 Day — Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, or Fri. An empty-cell click fills the day you clicked.
5 Room — “No room”, or a classroom. The label shows the room name and, if set, the building.
6 Starts and Ends — required. An empty-cell click fills that period’s times. End must be later than start.
7 Click Add lesson. Confirm “Add this lesson to the week?”. Close, Cancel, Escape, or a click on the dim background closes without saving.

Edit

Click a filled cell. The title becomes Edit lesson. The same fields open with the current values. Save lesson asks “Save changes to this lesson?”. The lesson stays on the same section.

Delete

Trash on the cell. Confirm the delete. This removes one lesson only.

Saving checks three clashes for that day. A clash is any overlap of the clock times, not only an exact match:

  • This section already has a lesson in that time — “This slot overlaps an existing timetable entry”.
  • This teacher already teaches any class in that time — “This teacher is already teaching at that time”.
  • This room name is already used in that time — “This room is already booked at that time”. No room means the room check is skipped.

Editing your own lesson does not clash with itself. Two lessons may touch, such as 08:10 end and 08:10 start. They clash if one starts before the other ends.

Generate week

Headmaster and Academic master. You need a year and a section, and the week must be unlocked. The button asks: “Replace this week with lessons from Academics allocations?”

Confirming deletes every lesson already on this section, then writes a new week. If some lessons cannot be placed, they are simply missing. Unlock is not enough to get the old week back. Copy anything you must keep, or add it again by hand after generate.

Generate looks at allocations for this year and this class. An allocation for the whole class is included. An allocation for this section is included. An allocation for a different section of the same class is skipped.

For each allocation it tries to place five lessons across Monday–Friday, using lesson bell periods only (not breaks). It walks days and periods in order and skips a candidate when:

  • The teacher has teacher hours, and this period is not fully inside a window on that day. A teacher with no hours is treated as free all day.
  • The same subject would sit in the period immediately before or after on that same day. Back-to-back doubles are avoided. A gap is allowed.
  • The section, the teacher, or the chosen room is already busy, including lessons already on other classes.

A room is chosen from the school’s classrooms when any exist. If both the room and the section have a capacity, Generate prefers a room at least as large as the section. If none is large enough, it still uses a room. Lessons can be saved with no room when the school has no classrooms.

Generate does not open the lesson form and does not ask again for each cell. When it finishes, the grid reloads. The lesson count is whatever was placed, which can be less than five per subject if the week ran out of legal slots.

Lock and unlock

Lock applies to the selected section and the selected year. Other sections are not locked by this click.

1 Pick the year and section.
2 Click Lock. Confirm “Lock this week? No one can change slots until you unlock.”
3 The Locked chip appears. Add lesson disappears. Generate is disabled. Cells no longer open the form. Trash disappears. The subtitle says click a cell to view.
4 Click Unlock and confirm “Unlock so this week can be edited?” The chip goes away and editing returns.

A locked week rejects add, edit, delete, and generate with “This timetable is locked”, even if a button were forced. Bell times and teacher hours are not locked by this switch. They are school-wide and stay editable.

Bell times

Headmaster and Academic master. Tab Bell times. The note: these rows appear on every class week. A lesson period is a teaching row. A break is rest time, not a lesson. Drag a row by its handle to change the order.

Bells are not per class. Reordering or editing P1 changes the rows on Form 1 A and Form 4 B alike. Lessons already saved keep their own start times. After you change a bell’s clock time, a lesson still appears on the row whose start matches the lesson, not on the row that used to have that name.

Each row

The row shows the name, then “Lesson period” or “Break”, then the clock times. On the right, three gold buttons:

  • Drag handle — three horizontal lines. Title “Drag up or down”. Press it, move onto another row, and release. That row takes the other row’s place. The week grid uses this order for lesson rows. Breaks stay in this same list order under the grid.
  • Pen — edit that bell.
  • Trash — delete. Confirm “Delete …?”.

Add or edit a period

1 Click Add period, or the pen. Title is “Add period” or “Edit period”. The note: name, type, and the start and end of this bell.
2 Name — required. Placeholder P1. Use a short label teachers will recognise.
3 Type — Lesson or Break.
4 Start and End — required. End must be after start, or you see “Period end must be after start”.
5 Add period or Save period. Confirm “Add this period to the school day?” or “Save changes to this period?”.

There is no separate “sort number” field. Order is the list order. Drag is how you change it. New periods are added at the end until you drag them.

Teacher hours

Headmaster and Academic master. Tab Teacher hours. Optional. If a teacher has hours, Generate only places them inside those windows. Leave a teacher off this list to treat them as free all day.

Hours do not block Add lesson. A hand-placed lesson can still sit outside the window. Only Generate obeys the windows.

The list is sorted by weekday, Monday at the top, then Friday. On the same day, earlier start times come first. Each row is the teacher’s name, then the short day, then from–to (Mon · 08:00–16:00).

Add teacher hours

1 Click Add teacher hours.
2 Teacher — required.
3 Day — Monday to Friday, or one of Mon, Tue, Wed, Thu, Fri. Monday to Friday is only on add, not on edit.
4 From and To — required. To must be after From, or you see “End time must be after start time”. The form opens at 08:00–16:00.
5 One day: button Add hours, confirm “Save these teacher hours?”. Monday to Friday: button Add Monday to Friday, confirm “Save these hours for Monday through Friday?”. That writes five rows, one per weekday, with the same times.

While Monday to Friday is selected, the form note says Generate only places this teacher inside this window on Monday through Friday. For a single day, the note says “on that day”.

Edit and delete

  • Pen — title “Edit teacher hours”. You change one saved row: teacher, one day, from, to. Save hours asks “Save changes to these hours?”. You cannot turn one row into the whole week from edit. Add again with Monday to Friday if you need all five.
  • Trash — confirm “Delete …?”. Removes that one window. If it was the teacher’s last window, Generate treats them as free all day again.

A window counts only when the whole period sits inside it. A period 07:30–08:10 is outside a window that starts at 08:00. A period 08:00–08:40 is inside 08:00–16:00. Several windows on the same day are allowed. Generate accepts the period if any one window on that day covers it completely.

If Monday to Friday saves only some days and then fails, the list refreshes so you can see the days that were stored. Delete the extras before you try again, or you will have two windows on those days.

What other people see

School admin

Timetable menu, Week tab only. Pick year and section, read cells, lock chip, lesson count, and breaks. No generate, lock, bells, or hours.

Teacher

Teacher home lists that teacher’s own lessons. They can also open Timetable and read any section, without edit buttons.

Student

Student home shows today’s timetable for their section. They do not open this admin page.

Attendance

A lesson log can be tied to a timetable slot for the section. The slot list follows what was saved on the week.

Staff and Invigilator match the teacher’s timetable menu: they can read a week and cannot change it.

Icons and confirms

  • Plus = add. Pen = edit. Trash = delete. Three lines = drag a bell row up or down.
  • Add lesson, Save lesson, Generate week, Lock, Unlock, delete lesson, save or delete a bell, and save or delete teacher hours all ask you to confirm.
  • Close, Cancel, Escape, or a click on the dim background leaves the form without saving.
  • The Week tab is one section. Bell times and teacher hours apply to the whole school.
