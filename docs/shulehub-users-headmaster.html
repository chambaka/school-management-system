ShuleHub Users menu

Headmaster guide · How to help people sign in

Sign in as Headmaster → left menu → Users

Add staff on Schools · Find login · Unlock · Reset password · Reset 2FA

Who this is for

This page is the school login list. Use it when someone cannot sign in. As Headmaster you can unlock accounts, reset passwords, and clear Google Authenticator. School admin and Academic master can do the same.

You do not create people here. Add staff and other officers from Schools → Officers (see below). Add a teacher on Teachers, a parent on Parents or during student admit, and a student on Students. They then appear on Users.

Open the page

1 Sign in with your Headmaster email or phone.
2 If you have more than one school, pick the school first (Schools).
3 Click Users in the left menu. Title: “Users”. Subtitle: “Every login at this school.”

Find a person

1 Open Users.
2 Type in Search users if you know part of the name, email, or phone.
3 Use the Role list if you know their job (for example Teacher or Parent).
4 Phone is under the name — “No phone” means a password reset cannot SMS them.

The list shows everyone at this school: officers, teachers, staff, parents, and students. Platform admin accounts do not appear here.

Add staff

Staff is a school officer, not a teacher. Add them from Schools, not from Users.

1 Left menu → Schools.
2 On the school card, click Officers. Title: “School officers”.
3 Click Add officer.
4 Enter Name and Email.
5 Set Role to Staff.
6 Enter a phone, or set a password. If the password is blank, ShuleHub creates a temporary one and tries to SMS it.
7 Click Save officer and confirm. They then appear on Users with role Staff.
Teachers are different: add those on Teachers, not Officers. The same Officers form also adds Headmaster, School admin, Academic master, Accountant, and Invigilator — only the Role changes.

Unlock a locked login

After five wrong passwords, ShuleHub locks the account for 15 minutes. The row shows a Locked chip. Unlock now if they need to get in before that time ends.

1 Find the row with the Locked chip.
2 Click Unlock.
3 Confirm. They can sign in again at once. Failed-attempt count is cleared.
Unlock does not change their password. If they forgot it, use Reset password as well.

Reset a password

1 Find the person. Check they have a phone under the name.
2 Click Reset password.
3 Confirm. ShuleHub creates a temporary password. If they have a phone, it tries to SMS it.

A successful reset also unlocks them and signs them out of other devices. Tell them to change the temporary password after they sign in.

If the row says “No phone”, Reset password fails. Open their page (Students, Teachers, Parents, or Schools → Officers), add a phone, then come back to Users — or tell them a new password in person after you add the phone and reset again.

Reset 2FA (Google Authenticator)

Shows only if the row has a 2FA chip. Use this when they lost their phone or cannot open the authenticator app.

1 Click Reset 2FA.
2 Confirm. Their authenticator is cleared.
3 They sign in with email/phone and password, then set up Google Authenticator again if the school still requires 2FA.

Active and Disabled

StatusMeaningWhat you do
ActiveThey can sign in (unless Locked).Unlock / reset as needed.
DisabledThe account was turned off (for example a student was deactivated).Restore them on their own page (Students, Teachers, Parents, or Schools → Officers). Users does not restore.
LockedToo many failed logins.Unlock on this page.
2FAAuthenticator is enrolled.Reset 2FA if they cannot complete the code.
