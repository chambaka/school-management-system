ShuleHub Students menu

Headmaster guide · How to run the school register

Sign in as Headmaster → left menu → Students

Admit · Address · Insurance · Place · Promote

Who this is for

This page is the school register. As Headmaster you can do every action on it. School admin and Academic master can do the same. Teachers and accountants can open the list but cannot admit, suspend, or promote.

Set up Academic years and Classes (with sections) under Academics before you admit students. A student can be admitted or edited with No year and No class. Promotion needs a target year and class when you move them.

Open the page

1 Sign in with your Headmaster email or phone.
2 If you have more than one school, pick the school first (Schools).
3 Click Students in the left menu. Title: “Directory and admission”.

Admit a student

1 Click Admit student.
2 Enter First name, Last name, and email. Middle name is optional. Nationality is a dropdown and is required. Tanzanian is already selected; change it only if the student is not Tanzanian.
3 Enter a phone number (Tanzania format). If you leave password blank, ShuleHub creates a temporary password and tries to SMS it to that phone.
4 Year can stay No year. Class can stay No class until you place them. If you pick a class, you can also pick a section or leave No section. Gender, roll number, date of birth, and admission date are optional.
5 Complete the address (required). See Address below. Emergency contact, blood group, insurance, and medical notes are optional.
6 Optional: upload a photo (JPEG, PNG, or WebP, max 2 MB).
7 Optional: tick Create parent now (see below).
8 Click Save student and confirm. The admission number is assigned automatically. If you ticked Create parent now, the parent account and the link are created in the same save.
If SMS is not configured, the student (and parent) are still created. Give them the password another way. Email cannot be changed after admit — edit the rest of the record instead. Save is blocked until region, district, ward, and street are filled in.

Create a parent during admission

Use this when the guardian is new. You do not need to open the Parents page first.

1 On the Admit student form, tick Create parent now.
2 Enter parent name and email (both required).
3 Enter a parent phone, or set a parent password. If you leave the password blank, ShuleHub creates a temporary password and tries to SMS it.
4 Choose the relationship: Father, Mother, Guardian, or Other. Occupation is optional. Parent address is required, in the same way as the student: region, district, ward, and street. P.O. Box is optional.
5 Save student. The parent is linked as primary contact, emergency contact, and invoice recipient.
Save stays disabled until the parent has a name, email, and either a phone or a strong password. The parent address must also be complete. If the parent email is already registered, the whole admit is rejected — change the email or link an existing parent after save.

Edit a student

  1. Click the gold pen icon on the row.
  2. Change names, nationality, phone, address, emergency contact, blood group, insurance, photo, notes, year, class, or section. Address still needs region, district, ward, and street.
  3. To take them out of a year, choose No year. To take them out of a class, choose No class (section clears). To keep the class and drop only the stream, choose No section.
  4. Click Save changes and confirm.

Admission number stays on the form header so you can see who you are editing. Email cannot be changed here.

Saving No year or No class really clears that placement. The list shows — instead of a class name. Place them again with Edit, or with Promote on that row.

Link a parent later

If you did not create a parent at admit, or the parent already exists:

  1. If they are new, add them under Parents first.
  2. On a student with “No parent”, click Link.
  3. Choose the parent and the relationship (Father, Mother, Guardian, Other).
  4. Click Link parent.

To remove the link, click Unlink and confirm. The parent account is not deleted.

Keep a student login safe

Unlock

Shows when the row has a Locked chip (five failed logins). Confirm Unlock so they can sign in again at once.

Reset password

Creates a temporary password. If they have a phone, ShuleHub sends it by SMS. Confirm before you click.

Reset 2FA

Shows only if they enrolled Google Authenticator. After reset they set up 2FA again at next sign-in.

Suspend

Active students only. They cannot sign in. Use this for a short stop, not a leaver.

Deactivate and restore

  1. Deactivate archives the student. They leave the normal list. Data stays.
  2. Tick Show archived to find them.
  3. Restore brings a suspended or archived student back to Active.

Deactivate is for leavers you may need again. Prefer Suspend if they will return this term.

Promote, repeat, graduate, or transfer

There is no Promote button at the top of the page. Open the form from the student’s row. The form already knows who they are — you do not pick a name again.

1 On an Active student, click Promote. The form title is their name. The subtitle shows admission number and current class.
2 Choose an action: Promote (next class), Repeat (same class; next academic year if you leave year blank), Graduate, or Transfer.
3 Choose year, class, and section for the new placement. Add a short note if useful. Close, Cancel, Escape, or a click outside closes the form without applying.
4 Click Preview. Check From → To.
5 If the preview is right, click Apply and confirm. History is written. The form then closes. Repeat on the next student if you are moving a class one by one.
Always Preview before Apply. Apply cannot be undone from this screen — you would edit the student or Promote again to correct a mistake.

If something fails

What you seeWhat to do
Empty class or year listsOpen Academics and create the year, class, and section first.
Cannot save a new studentNeed first name, last name, nationality, region, district, ward, street, and a phone or a password that meets the strength bar. If Create parent now is ticked, parent name, email, the same address fields, and phone or password are also required.
Region, district, or ward list is emptyThe location service is not available. Wait until the lists load, then choose region, then district, then ward.
Old address will not saveIt is sitting in Street as plain text. Choose region, district, and ward, then save again.
Insurance provider is not in the listChoose Add provider…, type the name, and click Add.
Email is already registeredThe parent (or student) email is in use. Use another email, or admit without Create parent now and Link the existing parent.
Photo rejectedUse JPEG/PNG/WebP under 2 MB.
“No SMS was sent”Student still saved or password still reset. Check the phone number, or tell them the password in person.
Parent list empty when linkingAdd the parent on the Parents page first. Archived parents do not appear.
Year or class still there after I chose No year / No classRestart the API if it was already running, then Save changes again.
Promote form not on the pageThat is expected. Click Promote on the student’s row.
