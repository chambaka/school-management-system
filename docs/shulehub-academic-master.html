ShuleHub academic master

What you can open after an academic master signs in: the home snapshot, the side menu, and the academic work on each page.

Academic master login · October 2026

Sign in

  1. Open the school portal.
  2. Enter the email, phone, or username the school gave you.
  3. Enter your password.
  4. Click Login.

A correct academic master login opens Home. The gold line above the greeting says Admin cockpit. The heading is “Good day” and your name. Under it is the school name and “live snapshot.”

Five wrong passwords lock the account for 15 minutes. Forgot password sends an SMS code to the phone on the account.

This guide is for an academic master login. A teacher, accountant, or headmaster has a different set of menus. There is no Finance item in the academic master menu, and there is no Officers or Branding item.

Home

The snapshot cards are Students, Teachers, Invoices, Notices, Exams, Pending approvals, Unpublished exams, Absent today, and Fees outstanding. Invoices and Fees outstanding are counts only. They do not open a fee ledger.

Today’s tasks lists what the school snapshot says is waiting. If nothing is waiting, it says “Nothing waiting right now.” Under the list are links to Attendance, Exams, and Timetable.

Alerts shows assignment posted, up to five assignment submissions, then other unread alerts such as results published. Each line has a date. All alerts opens Reports, where the full list sits at the bottom and unread rows can be marked read.

Students

The page is the student directory. Search by name or admission details, and sort with the Name, Email, and Status controls.

  • Admit student creates the student record and login. A password can be typed, or left blank so a temporary password is sent by SMS to the phone.
  • Edit updates class, section, and the other admission fields. You can upload a photo (JPEG, PNG, or WebP, up to 2 MB).
  • Link attaches a parent and a relationship: father, mother, guardian, or other.
  • Promote previews the move, then applies promote, repeat, graduate, or transfer. That action is what writes a row in Enrolment history. Admitting a student does not.
  • You can unlock a locked login, reset 2FA, reset the password by SMS, archive the student, or restore an archived one. Show archived is on the page head.

Users

Users lists every login at this school. Filter by role, then sort by name, email, role, or status.

For someone other than yourself you can Unlock, Reset 2FA when authenticator is enrolled, Reset password (SMS if they have a phone), and Disable or Enable sign-in. Remove deletes the login together with its allocations and links. You cannot disable your own login from this list.

Teachers

The gold label above the heading says Staff directory. Each card shows the teacher’s name with the phone number on the same line, then employee ID, department, and email, then status chips such as locked or 2FA.

Name, Email, and Status are gold buttons. Click one to sort. Click it again to reverse the order. The list starts sorted by name.

Add teacher saves the person, optional password, phone, department, qualification, and specialization. Qualifications come from the platform configuration. On an existing teacher you can change the photo, see subject allocations, remove an allocation, unlock, reset 2FA, reset the password, archive, or restore.

Parents

Parents is the guardian list. Add a parent, edit the record, and see which students are linked. Linking a child to a parent is also done from the student card.

Academics

Academics is a set of tabs. You can add and edit on each tab except buildings.

Timetable

Choose the year and section. The week grid shows lessons in the bell periods. Click an empty cell to add a lesson, or a filled cell to edit it, unless the timetable is locked.

  • Generate week places lessons from the teacher allocations. A teacher who has hours is only placed inside those windows. A teacher with no hours is treated as free all day.
  • Bell times are the rows on every class week. A lesson row is teaching time. A break is rest, not a lesson. Drag a row to change the order.
  • Teacher hours are optional windows. Generate uses them when they exist.

Exams

Pick the academic year, then Create exam for a class. The life of an exam is draft, entered, verified, approved, then published.

  1. The allocated teacher adds the papers and enters marks on Grades. A paper can be added only by the teacher allocated to that subject for the class.
  2. When the status is Entered, you Verify. Marks freeze at verification.
  3. When the status is Verified, you Approve.
  4. Publish is available after approval. Students and parents then see the results, and they get an in-app alert. Parents who prefer SMS, and who have a phone, are texted. You can Unpublish later. Unpublish hides the exam again and sets the status back to approved. Alerts that were already sent stay.
  5. Reject sends the exam back so marks can be edited again.

You can lock the schedule, and you can delete an exam after it is unpublished and marks are removed if the page asks for that. Verify, approve, and publish stay disabled until the exam has papers.

Grades

Choose the exam and subject. Save grid writes the marks. Download Excel template is the sheet for those marks.

You can save marks only for a subject you are allocated to teach for that class. Otherwise the page says only the allocated teacher can submit marks. After verification the grid is frozen until the exam is rejected on Exams.

Term Results opens the combined midterm, semi-terminal, and terminal scores. A paper that is missing or not published counts as 0 in that combined score.

Assignments

An assignment moves from Draft to Locked to Published.

  • Add one with class, optional section, subject, title, instructions, and due date. A new assignment is a draft.
  • You can edit or delete any assignment, including one a teacher can no longer change.
  • Lock stops the teacher from editing or deleting it.
  • Publish shows it to the students and parents in that class, and raises an “Assignment posted” alert.
  • On a published assignment, View work opens the submissions. Enter CA marks appears only when you are the allocated teacher of that subject. Students who have not submitted are not in the mark list. Save marks is per submission.

Documents on the assignment are the files the teacher attached. The refresh icon reloads documents and submissions.

Attendance

Three tabs: Students, Staff, and Lesson logs.

  • Students: choose the date, section, and lesson, mark each student, then Save register. Absent students trigger a parent SMS when that parent still receives SMS.
  • Staff: mark teachers and save the staff register.
  • Lesson logs: the record of lessons taught for the section.

Reports

Download stays locked until the on-screen list matches the filters you have now. Change a filter and you must click Show again before PDF, CSV, or Excel will run.

ReportWhat it shows
Enrolment historyPromote, repeat, graduate, and transfer. The class and year are the placement at the time of that action, which is the class they left. Admitting a student does not create a row.
Term resultCombined midterm, semi-terminal, and terminal scores for one student or every active student.
Report cardOne published exam: subject marks, grade, percentage, GPA, and position. Download report card is that exam. Download combined term scores is only the combined table.
School reportsMerit list for an exam, fee defaulters, fee collections for a date range, or attendance for a date range. These fee reports live here. They are not a Finance menu.
AlertsThe full notification list. Mark read on an unread row.

Messages

Choose a student. The thread is comments about that student. Parents see it and can reply. Write comment posts your note. You can tick the option to send the same note to parents by SMS. The thread then shows how many texts were sent, failed, or skipped.

Notices

Notices are the school board, not alerts, and they are not sent by SMS or email. People see them only after they sign in.

Publish notice needs a title, a body, and an audience: All, Teachers, Students, Parents, or Staff. The form publishes immediately. It does not schedule a start, set an expiry, or target one class. The page has no edit or delete. A teacher sees All, Teachers, and Staff. A student sees All and Students. A parent sees All and Parents.

Audit

Audit trail lists what was done at the school. Filter by correction ID or by action, and search the rows on the page.

Each row has when, the full Correction ID, the actor, the action, the resource, and the summary. The correction ID is the same value the portal sends with a request, so a support note can be matched to the row. It wraps onto more than one line so the whole ID stays visible.

Security

Enter the current password and a new one that meets the strength rules, then Update password. Other sessions are signed out.

Under Notification channels, click In-app or SMS. The one you click stays gold.

  • In-app keeps alerts in the portal and stops SMS for your account.
  • SMS sends your alerts by text and stops the in-app copy.

Email and push are not offered. Until you choose, alerts stay as they are today: in the app, and by SMS where the school already texts. If Google Authenticator is enrolled on your account, Security also shows Reset 2FA.

A normal week

  1. Sign in and read Home: unpublished exams, pending approvals, and absent today.
  2. Open Exams and move anything that is entered through verify and approve, then publish when the marks should be seen.
  3. Open Assignments and lock or publish drafts that are ready.
  4. Check Attendance if a register is still open, then Timetable if the week needs generating.
  5. Use Reports when you need a term result, a report card, a merit list, or the enrolment history. Show the list before you download it.
  6. Publish a notice when the whole school, or one audience, needs a board message.
  7. Sign out on a shared computer.
