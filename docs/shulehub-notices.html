ShuleHub notices

How the school board works: who can publish, who can read, and where a notice appears after sign-in.

School board · Not the alert inbox · October 2026

What a notice is

A notice is a message pinned on the school board inside ShuleHub. It has a title, a body, and an audience. People read it after they sign in. Publishing a notice does not text anyone, email anyone, or drop a row into the alert list.

Use a notice for something the school wants a group to read when they next open the portal: a meeting, a uniform reminder, a fee deadline, a timetable change, a holiday.

Alerts are a different system. An alert is created by the platform itself, for example when an assignment is posted, a student submits work, or exam results are published. Those appear under Alerts on the home page, and some of them also send SMS. A notice never does that.

People must sign in

There is no public notice page. A person who is not logged in cannot see the board. There is also no copy sent to a phone or an inbox outside the portal.

After sign-in, a matching notice shows in two places:

  • Home. Titles only. A student or a teacher sees the first four. A parent sees every matching title. The teacher home also shows a count of the notices that teacher can read.
  • Notices in the side menu. The full title and body, newest first, with the author name and the audience.

The school home used by the headmaster, school admin, academic master, and accountant shows a notice count. That count is every notice stored for the school.

Who can publish

These roles see the publish form on the Notices page:

RoleCan publishWhat they read
Headmaster Yes Every notice in the school, including any that is not published.
School admin Yes Every notice in the school, including any that is not published.
Academic master Yes Every notice in the school, including any that is not published.
Teacher Yes Published notices for All, Teachers, and Staff. A notice aimed only at students or only at parents is hidden from the teacher.
Accountant Yes Every published notice, whichever audience it uses.
Staff Yes Every published notice, whichever audience it uses.
Student No Published notices for All and Students.
Parent No Published notices for All and Parents.

An invigilator does not have Notices in the side menu. Students and parents have the menu item, but they only see the list. The publish form is not on their page.

How to publish

  1. Sign in with a role that can write notices.
  2. Open Notices in the side menu.
  3. Enter a Title. This is the line people see on the home page.
  4. Enter a Body. This is the full message on the Notices page.
  5. Choose an Audience. The default is All.
  6. Click Publish notice and confirm.

The notice is live at once. It is marked Published, your name is stored as the author, and it appears at the top of the board for everyone in that audience. The title and body boxes clear so you can write the next one.

Search on the Notices page matches the title, the body, the audience, the author, and the word published.

The screen does not save a draft. Publish notice always posts the message. There is no Edit button and no Delete button on the page. Plan the wording before you confirm.

Choosing an audience

The audience is the whole group in this school. It is not one class, one stream, or one child.

A teacher who publishes a Students or Parents notice will not see that notice in their own list afterwards. The headmaster, school admin, and academic master still see it. The students or parents it was written for see it after they sign in.

Where each person looks

Student

On the student home, the Notices card lists up to four titles. Open Notices in the side menu to read the body. Only All and Students notices appear. A Parents-only or Teachers-only notice is not on their board.

Parent

On the parent home, the Notices card lists every matching title. Open Notices for the body. Only All and Parents notices appear. A message written only for students is not shown to the parent.

Teacher

The teacher home shows a Notices count and up to four titles. Open Notices for the full board and to publish. The teacher sees All, Teachers, and Staff. They do not see a notice whose audience is only Students or only Parents.

Headmaster, school admin, and academic master

They use the same Notices page to publish and to read. Their list is the full school board, not filtered by audience. The school home count includes every stored notice.

Examples

Friday parents’ meeting

Audience: Parents. Title: Parents’ meeting, Friday 4 pm. Body: the hall, the time, and what to bring.

A parent sees the title at home and the full text under Notices. A student does not. A teacher does not see it on their own board. The headmaster still sees it.

School closes for a public holiday

Audience: All. Everyone who can open Notices sees it: students, parents, teachers, staff, and school leaders.

Reminder to submit marks

Audience: Teachers. Teachers see it. Students and parents do not. Staff and the accountant also see published notices, so they will see this one too.

Sports day kit for students

Audience: Students. Students see it after they sign in. Parents do not, unless you also publish a separate notice with audience Parents. One notice cannot be sent to two audiences at once. All is the way to reach both.

What the board does not do

  • It does not send SMS.
  • It does not send email.
  • It does not create an alert. People are not told that a new notice exists until they open the portal.
  • It does not target one class, one section, or one student. Audience is a role group for the whole school.
  • It does not expire on a date. A published notice stays on the board.
  • It does not schedule a future publish time from this screen. Publish notice posts now.
  • The page has no edit and no delete. Check the title, body, and audience before you confirm.

Checklist before you publish

  1. Is this a board message, or should it be an alert or an SMS? A notice waits until the person logs in.
  2. Will the title alone make sense on the home page? Home shows the title, not the body.
  3. Is the audience the whole group you mean? Students and parents need All, or two separate notices.
  4. Are you a teacher posting only to students or parents? You will not see that notice in your own list. A school leader will.
  5. Is the wording final? This screen does not take the notice down.
