ShuleHub yearly subscriptions

White-label school SaaS · Chambaka · September 2026

Charge per school, paid in advance for 12 months. SMS, custom domain, and dedicated installs sit on top.

Basic — run the register.   Standard — run the school.   Professional — run the group.   Enterprise — run it as your own system.

Commercial model

Schools already plan in academic years. One yearly invoice is easier for a board to approve than twelve monthly ones. Cash comes in up front; you suspend if they do not renew.

Item Rule
Unit of saleOne school. A group with four schools buys four subscriptions (with a volume discount), not one vague platform licence.
Term12 months. Align to their academic year when you can (for example 1 October – 30 September).
Payment100% in advance (bank or mobile money). Termly only if they cannot pay annually — then charge 10–15% more than the yearly price.
IncludesHosting, updates, email support, and the modules in that package. One branding pack (name, colours, logo) from Professional up.
Extra (always billed separately)SMS bundles, custom domain (if not in the package), on-site training, data import, extra storage.
Control todayTenant and school status: Active, Suspended, Archived. Data stays on suspend; they cannot work until they pay.
Do not split “academics plan” vs “finance plan” in year one. One full school product, gated by package. Do not bury SMS in the yearly fee — one busy school will wipe the margin.

How a subscription year runs

1Quote and payThey pay. You create the tenant and school, add officers, set status to Active.
2Paid throughEnd date = pay date + 12 months, or the academic year you agreed.
3RenewInvoice next year 30 days before expiry.
4Grace14–30 days after expiry if unpaid.
5SuspendOrganization or that school. Data stays. They cannot work.
6RestoreThey pay. You set Active again.

How to sell it

Lead with Standard. Show Basic only if they cannot pay for fees and exams. Show Professional when they say “we have another campus.” Show Enterprise only in a meeting.

Until plan flags are enforced in the product, sell packages on paper and turn Professional extras (extra schools, branding, SMS) on by hand. Existing fields: subscriptionPlan, financeEnabled, attendanceEnabled, examsEnabled, custom domain, and single-tenant mode.
