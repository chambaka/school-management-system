ShuleHub roles

What each role is for, and which part of the platform that person can open.

Platform · Organization · School

Three levels

A person has one role. The role decides the menus on the left and what the buttons on those screens will do.

LevelWhoWhat they look after
Platform Platform admin Every organization. Creates the organization and its first organization admin.
Organization Organization admin One organization: its profile, its schools, and the officers of those schools.
School Headmaster and everyone below One school: people, teaching, exams, fees, and the day-to-day screens.
The Platform admin creates an organization admin. The organization admin appoints the Headmaster and the School admin for each school. The Headmaster and the School admin appoint the other school officers: Academic master, Teacher, Accountant, Staff, and Invigilator. Parents and students are created from the school’s people screens.

Platform admin

Whole platform

Opens on Tenants

  • Create an organization, then suspend it, activate it, or archive it.
  • Add a school to any organization, and open Officers for any school.
  • Create organization admins. This is the only role that can create them.
  • Config: shared qualification list, how many terms a new school year uses, Google Authenticator for each school, and which actions are written to the audit.
  • Users across the platform, and the platform audit.
  • Security: own password and sign-in code.

Menus: Tenants, Schools, Config, Users, Audit, Security.

On a single-organization install, the same account also sees the Headmaster school menus, plus Schools and Audit for that organization. Config stays.

Organization admin

One organization

Opens on Schools

  • Organization profile: name, email, phone, country, timezone, currency, and terms per year (2 or 4).
  • Add a school, rename it, suspend it, or activate it again. A suspended school cannot sign in.
  • Overview for each school: students, teachers, exams not yet published, and outstanding fees.
  • Officers for a school, including Headmaster and School admin.
  • Audit of organization profile, school, and officer changes.
  • Security: own password and sign-in code.

Menus: Schools, Audit, Security.

This role does not open a school dashboard, and does not create another organization or another organization admin.

Headmaster

One school

Opens on Home

  • Admit and manage students, parents, and teachers. Users and Officers for this school.
  • Academics: years, terms, classes, subjects, buildings, rooms, and grading bands.
  • Timetable: lessons, bell times, and teacher hours.
  • Exams: create, papers, schedule, send for checking, reject, approve, and publish. Verify is the Academic master’s step.
  • Grades: enter marks, download the Excel template, import marks, and prepare term results.
  • Assignments, and the staff attendance register. Student attendance is entered by the Academic master and teachers.
  • Finance is view: fee structures, invoices, and the student ledger. Creating fees, generating invoices, recording payments, and discounts stay with the Accountant.
  • Reports, messages, notices, branding, and the school audit.

Menus: Home, Students, Users, Officers, Teachers, Parents, Academics, Timetable, Exams, Grades, Assignments, Attendance, Finance, Reports, Messages, Notices, Branding, Audit, Security.

School admin

One school

Opens on Home

  • Same people work as the Headmaster: students, parents, teachers, users, and officers.
  • Academics, including buildings and rooms. Grading bands stay with the Headmaster and the Academic master.
  • Timetable and exams are open to look at. This role does not edit the timetable and does not create, verify, approve, or publish exams.
  • No Grades menu, so this role does not enter marks.
  • Staff attendance register. The student register is entered by the Academic master and teachers.
  • Finance is view: fee structures and the student ledger.
  • Reports, messages, notices, branding, and the school audit.

Menus: Home, Students, Users, Officers, Teachers, Parents, Academics, Timetable, Exams, Assignments, Attendance, Finance, Reports, Messages, Notices, Branding, Audit, Security.

Academic master

Teaching operations

Opens on Home

  • Students, parents, teachers, and users.
  • Academics and grading bands. Buildings and rooms stay with the Headmaster and the School admin.
  • Timetable: lessons, bell times, and teacher hours.
  • Exams, including Verify. Also reject, approve, and publish, with the Headmaster.
  • Grades: enter marks, Excel import, and term results.
  • Student attendance and the staff register.
  • Assignments, reports, messages, notices, and the school audit. Finance is view.

Menus: Home, Students, Users, Teachers, Parents, Academics, Timetable, Exams, Grades, Assignments, Attendance, Finance, Reports, Messages, Notices, Audit, Security.

No Officers menu, no Branding, and no organization Schools page.

Teacher

Assigned classes

Opens on Home

  • See the student directory and the timetable.
  • Enter exam marks on Grades, including the Excel template and import, and send an exam for checking.
  • Create assignments and enter student attendance.
  • Reports, messages, and notices.

Menus: Home, Students, Timetable, Exams, Grades, Assignments, Attendance, Reports, Messages, Notices, Security.

Does not register students, manage teachers, edit the timetable, or open fees.

Accountant

School fees

Opens on Home

  • Student directory, used for billing.
  • Fee structures, including a different amount for one class.
  • Invoices, generate quarterly invoices, record a payment, give a discount, and the student ledger.
  • Reports, messages, notices, and the school audit.

Menus: Home, Students, Finance (Fee structures, Fee structure, Invoices, Generate quarterly invoices, Record payment, Discount, Student Ledger), Reports, Messages, Notices, Audit, Security.

Does not enter marks, edit the timetable, or take attendance.

Staff

School office

Opens on Notices

  • Student directory, the timetable, and attendance, to look up the day.
  • Write and read notices.

Menus: Students, Timetable, Attendance, Notices, Security.

Does not enter marks, edit the timetable, or manage fees.

Invigilator

Exam room

Opens on Exams

  • See the exam list, papers, and the timetable for the sitting.

Menus: Timetable, Exams, Security.

Does not create exams, enter marks, or manage people.

Parent

Own children

Opens on Home

  • Own children: classes, attendance, assignments, and the report card once results are published.
  • Fees and the student ledger for those children.
  • Messages to the school, and notices.

Menus: Home, Assignments, Finance, Messages, Notices, Report, Security.

Student

Own record

Opens on Home

  • Own home, including today’s timetable and attendance, assignments to hand in, invoices, notices, and the report card once results are published.

Menus: Home, Invoices, Assignments, Notices, Report, Security.
